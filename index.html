a cinematic god simulator · living 3D planets · infinite multiverse
🌍🪐✨⚡🧝👹🧚🐉💀👼⏳☄️

You are the One True God hovering over real 3D planets — spin them, zoom from orbit to street level, and strike anywhere on the sphere. Raise land, breathe life into 16 peoples (or design your own), and watch nations rise through the ages — stone to steel to social media — praying, warring, posting. Answer their prayers, anoint prophets, empower heroes, flood the world, walk your own heavens and hells, resurrect the dead, rewind time, and create or unmake entire planets — including doomed ones whose last child escapes by rocket.

  • 🖱 Left-drag / tap uses your selected power · ✋ pan · wheel/pinch zoom
  • 🪐 The Cosmos tab holds your planets · 👻 the Souls tab, your afterlife
  • 📖 The Testament tab is your story — hints inside if you want them
  • ⧏ The time dial runs backwards too (R) — far enough back and the world un-creates, and you speak it into being again
  • 💾 Autosaves; the world keeps turning while you're away
